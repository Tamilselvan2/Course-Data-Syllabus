Unit 1

Quality Control
& Concepts

QC vs QA, McCall’s model, CMM, SPICE, CASE tools, inspections, walkthroughs, and the Waterfall model.

🛡️
Quality Control (QC) vs Quality Assurance (QA)
Very Important

QC is product-oriented and reactive. It focuses on testing and verification to identify defects.

QA is process-oriented and proactive. It focuses on building quality into the process so defects are prevented.

Quality Control
  • Product testing and inspection
  • Reactive
  • Finds defects
  • Focused on the product
Quality Assurance
  • Process planning and standards
  • Proactive
  • Prevents defects
  • Focused on the process
📐
McCall’s Quality Model

Groups quality into three categories:

  • Product Operation — Correctness, Reliability, Efficiency, Integrity, Usability
  • Product Revision — Maintainability, Flexibility, Testability
  • Product Transition — Portability, Reusability, Interoperability

CMM / CMMI Levels
  1. Initial
    Chaotic and unpredictable.
  2. Repeatable
    Basic project management practices exist.
  3. Defined
    Processes are standardized and documented.
  4. Managed
    Processes are measured quantitatively.
  5. Optimizing
    Continuous improvement and defect prevention.
🔍
SPICE (ISO/IEC 15504)

Software Process Improvement and Capability Determination.

  • Level 0 — Incomplete
  • Level 1 — Performed
  • Level 2 — Managed
  • Level 3 — Established
  • Level 4 — Predictable
  • Level 5 — Optimizing
🤖
CASE Tools and Quality
  • Upper CASE — Requirements, design, modeling
  • Lower CASE — Coding, testing, debugging
  • Integrated CASE — Full SDLC support

Effect on quality: Better documentation, improved productivity, less human error, and higher consistency.

Waterfall Model & SQA

Requirements → Design → Implementation → Testing → Deployment → Maintenance

⚠️ SQA Catch

Testing happens only at the end, so a major design flaw can force large rework. For safety-critical systems, a more rigorous model like the V-Model is preferred.

🔀
FSM-Based Testing in Web Applications

FSM models states and transitions in user journeys.

Example: Browsing → Cart → Payment Gateway → Order Confirmed.

It helps ensure unexpected actions, such as using the back button during payment, are handled gracefully.

🎯
Total Quality Management (TQM)
Very Important

TQM is an organization-wide approach focused on customer satisfaction, continuous improvement, employee involvement, process orientation, leadership commitment, and data-driven decisions.

💰
Cost of Quality (COQ)
Repeated in Exams
Prevention Cost
Training, planning, standards, and process improvement to avoid defects.
Appraisal Cost
Testing, inspections, reviews, and audits used to detect defects.
Internal Failure
Rework, debugging, retesting, and scrap found before release.
External Failure
Complaints, support cost, warranty issues, and reputation loss after release.

Tools &
Methodologies

7 QC tools, Six Sigma, Zero Defect, BPR, QFD, Benchmarking, ISO 9000, SPICE, and SPC.

🧰
7 Basic QC Tools
Very Important
Fishbone Diagram
Identifies possible causes of a problem.
Pareto Chart
80/20 rule — prioritize the biggest causes.
Histogram
Shows frequency distribution of data.
Control Chart
Monitors process stability using UCL and LCL.
Scatter Diagram
Shows relationship between two variables.
Flow Chart
Represents process steps and workflow.
Check Sheet
Structured form for collecting quality data.
📉
Six Sigma — DMAIC

Goal: 3.4 defects per million opportunities.

  1. Define
    Identify problem and goals
  2. Measure
    Collect current performance data
  3. Analyze
    Find root causes
  4. Improve
    Implement solutions
  5. Control
    Sustain improvements
🎯
Zero Defect Philosophy

Do it right the first time. Prevention is better than correction, and every employee is responsible for quality.

🏭
BPR, QFD, Benchmarking, SPC
  • BPR — radical redesign of business processes
  • QFD — convert customer needs into technical requirements
  • Benchmarking — compare against best practices
  • SPC — statistical monitoring of process variation
🏅
ISO 9000 and SPICE
  • ISO 9000 — quality management system standards
  • SPICE — process capability and improvement framework
  • CMM Level 5 — highest maturity and continuous optimization

Defect Control
& Analysis

Root cause analysis, defect prevention, fault tolerance, failure containment, safety assurance, and hazard analysis.

🔎
Root Cause Analysis (RCA)

RCA finds the real underlying cause of a defect, not just the symptom. Tools include 5 Whys, Fishbone, and Pareto.

🛑
Defect Prevention Standards
  • Coding standards
  • Reviews and inspections
  • Testing standards
  • Training programs
  • Process improvement
🧱
Fault Tolerance & Failure Containment
  • Fault tolerance — system continues even if a part fails
  • Failure containment — limits the spread of failure
  • Techniques: redundancy, recovery, exception handling, isolation
🛡️
Safety Assurance & Damage Control
  • Hazard identification
  • Risk assessment
  • Safety testing
  • Documentation
  • Backup and recovery
