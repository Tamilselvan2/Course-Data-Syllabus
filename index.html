Module I

Introduction to Entrepreneurship

Concepts, definitions, economic development, classifications, traits, institutions, schemes, funding, and social entrepreneurship.

💡
Entrepreneurship – Definition
The process of starting a new business by identifying opportunities, organizing resources, taking risks, and earning profit. Involves innovation, employment creation, and solving market problems.
Core Concept
👤
Entrepreneur
A person who starts a business, takes risks, organizes factors of production, and introduces innovation. Examples: Elon Musk, Narayana Murthy, Ritesh Agarwal (OYO).
Core Concept
🎯
Traits of Successful Entrepreneurs
Risk-taking ability, innovation, leadership, self-confidence, creativity, decision-making, hard work, goal-oriented, communication skills, adaptability, integrity, competitive spirit.
15 Marks
📈
Role in Economic Development
Employment generation, wealth creation, innovation, industrial development, balanced regional development, improved standard of living, increased exports, resource utilization, economic independence.
15 Marks
📊
Clarence Danhof's Classification
Innovative (new tech), Imitative (copies locally), Fabian (shy, passive until forced), Drone (refuses change even at loss). Also: Trading, Industrial, Agricultural, Social, Women, Serial, Portfolio.
15 Marks
🏛️
Entrepreneur vs Manager
Owner vs Employee. Takes risk vs Avoids risk. Innovation-focused vs Administration-focused. Creates business vs Runs business. Profit-oriented vs Salary-oriented.
5 Marks
💰
Venture Capitalists vs Angel Investors
VC: Firm/company, large money, growth stage, more control, formal. Angel: Individual, smaller amount, early stage, less control, personal investment.
15 Marks
🎓
E-Cells & EDP
E-Cells encourage startups, conduct workshops, organize hackathons, provide mentorship. EDPs develop entrepreneurial abilities through leadership and business skill training.
Core Concept
🤝
Social Entrepreneurship
Solving social problems through business: rural education, low-cost healthcare, eco-friendly products. Benefits: social welfare, employment, rural development.
Core Concept
🇮🇳
Government Schemes
Startup India (tax exemption, funding), Stand-Up India (SC/ST & women), MUDRA Loan (micro funding), PMEGP (employment generation), SIDBI (MSME credit), DPIIT framework.
Very Important
📜
Historical Definitions
Etymology: French "Entreprendre" (to undertake). Schumpeter: "Creative Destruction." Drucker: Shifts resources to higher productivity. Primary motivation: independence.
Bonus Marks
🏗️
Institutional Support
NSIC (marketing & technical support), KVIC (rural employment), Incubators (mentorship, workspace, resource mapping). DPIIT grants self-certification for 3 years.
Core Concept
Module II

Development of Business Model

Innovation, creativity, business models, value propositions, feasibility analysis, business plans, and project management tools.

🧠
Innovation & Creativity
Innovation: Implementation of new ideas. Creativity: Generation of new ideas. Importance: competitive advantage, customer satisfaction, growth, market survival.
Core Concept
⚠️
Challenges of Innovation
Lack of funds, market uncertainty, technology changes, competition, customer acceptance, high risk.
5 Marks
🧩
Business Model
Explains how a business creates, delivers, and captures value. Developed by Alexander Osterwalder. 9 building blocks. HR Policy is NOT one of them.
15 Marks
🎨
Business Model Canvas
1. Customer Segments 2. Value Proposition 3. Channels 4. Customer Relationships 5. Revenue Streams 6. Key Activities 7. Key Resources 8. Key Partners 9. Cost Structure
15 Marks
💎
Value Proposition
Why customers should buy your product. The central core of BMC. Examples: faster delivery, lower price, better quality, eco-friendly product.
15 Marks
🔍
Opportunity Analysis
Identifying market gap, customer need, and business opportunity. Sources: customers, competitors, technology, market trends, existing problems.
Core Concept
✅
Feasibility Analysis
Checks if idea is practical and profitable. Types: Technical (resources), Financial (profitability), Market (demand), Organizational (management), Scheduling (timeline).
15 Marks
📋
Business Plan Components
Executive Summary (written last, placed first), Company Description, Market Analysis, Product/Service Description, Marketing Plan, Operational Plan, Financial Plan, Funding Requirement, Appendix.
15 Marks
📐
SWOT & BEP
SWOT: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats. Break-Even Point (BEP): Volume where total revenue equals total cost. Lean Startup: MVP with Build-Measure-Learn loop.
Very Important
🛠️
Project Management Tools
Gantt Chart, PERT, CPM, Jira, Zoho Projects, Kanban Board, Work Breakdown Structure (WBS), Smartsheet, Microsoft Project.
15 Marks
⏱️
Critical Path Method (CPM)
Identifies longest sequence of dependent tasks determining minimum project duration. Any delay here delays final delivery. Reduces delay, helps scheduling.
5 Marks
🔄
Project Development Cycle
Initiation → Planning → Execution → Monitoring & Controlling → Closure. Or: Idea generation → Feasibility study → Planning → Implementation → Monitoring → Completion.
Core Concept
❌
Fatal Flaws of Business Model
No customer need, weak revenue model, poor market research, high cost, lack of differentiation, wrong pricing, poor management, undefined value proposition.
5 Marks
💳
Revenue Models
Subscription (predictable income), Freemium (free basic, paid premium), Retailer, Manufacturer, Fee-for-Service, Product-as-a-Service, Leasing, Razor-and-Blade.
Very Important
Module III

Transformation into Startup

Startup culture, marketing intelligence, customer segments, channels, revenue streams, financial analysis, legal aspects, ethical culture, and strategic improvement.

🚀
Startup Culture in India
Growth drivers: Digital India, internet growth, government support, funding availability, youth innovation, Startup India initiative. Advantages: innovation, employment, economic growth.
15 Marks
🧭
Market Intelligence
Collection of market-related information: competitor analysis, customer behavior, market trends, pricing analysis. Methods: surveys, focus groups, interviews, ethical competitor intelligence.
Core Concept
👥
Customer Segments
Mass market, niche market, local market, global market. Segmentation divides broad customer base into distinct groups based on shared characteristics for tailored value propositions.
Very Important
📡
Channels & Partners
Paths to deliver value: website, retail stores, delivery apps, social media. Partners: VARs, MSPs, OEMs, strategic partners. Mass marketing (TV, high spend) vs Niche marketing (digital, low cost).
Core Concept
💵
Revenue Streams
Product sales, subscription, advertising, licensing, commission, freemium model. How money is earned. Very frequently tested in exams.
15 Marks
📢
Effective Marketing Strategies
Social media marketing, influencer marketing, email marketing, SEO, referral marketing, discounts/offers.
5 Marks
📊
Financial Performance Analysis
Evaluates profit, sales, expenses, cash flow. Cash flow projection: most vital early-stage statement. Balance sheet: assets, liabilities, equity. Asset-to-liability ratio below 1:1 = bankruptcy risk.
Core Concept
🔥
Burn Rate & Bootstrapping
Burn rate: rate at which pre-revenue startup spends cash reserves monthly. Bootstrapping: starting and growing using personal savings and early revenues without external venture funding.
Bonus Marks
⚖️
Legal Aspects of Startups
Sole Proprietorship (unlimited liability), Partnership, LLP (flexibility + limited liability), Private Limited Company (min 2 directors, Companies Act 2013), IBC 2016 (fast exit for failed startups).
15 Marks
📜
Mandatory Licenses & Permits
GST Registration, Udyam/MSME Registration, Shop & Establishment Act License, FSSAI License (food sector), IPR: Patents, Trademarks, Copyrights.
Very Important
🛡️
Ethical Culture
Honesty, transparency, fair treatment, social responsibility. Sustained when leaders model desired behaviors. Importance: customer trust, brand image, employee satisfaction.
15 Marks
📈
Strategic Improvement Plan
Steps: identify problem, analyze market, improve product, reduce cost, increase marketing, monitor results. Scalability: grow revenue without proportional cost increase. Product-Market Fit: product satisfies strong market demand.
Core Concept
